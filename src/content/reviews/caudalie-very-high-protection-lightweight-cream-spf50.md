### What Is Caudalie Very High Protection Lightweight Cream SPF50+?

[Caudalie Very High Protection Lightweight Cream SPF50+](https://en.caudalie.com/p/533R1/very-high-protection-lightweight-cream-spf50-533r1.html) is a fragrance-free, water-resistant sunscreen for the face and décolleté. The 40 ml European formula offers SPF50+ and PA++++ protection and is marketed for sensitive skin, with an invisible finish that can double as a makeup base.

Despite the way it feels on my skin, this is not a mineral sunscreen. Its four “organic” filters—DHHB, ethylhexyl triazone, ensulizole, and bemotrizinol—are modern chemical filters. In European sunscreen language, “organic” describes the chemistry; it does not mean zinc oxide grown on a charming little farm.

### My Thoughts

As summer comes to an end, I am reminiscing about all the sunscreens I tried while searching for a replacement for my beloved, now-discontinued [Josie Maran Argan Daily Moisturizer SPF47](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=027821e6-db7d-4c03-956a-00313488ebc7). RIP. I bought several options at French pharmacies, sometimes in little travel-size gift sets that I cannot resist. At least this weakness was practical since I did a lot of traveling this summer.

I have already written about my disappointment with [La Roche-Posay Anthelios UVMune 400 Anti-Dark Spots Fluid SPF50+](https://theskinroutine.com/blog/la-roche-posay-anthelios-uvmune-400-anti-dark-spots-fluid-50/). The alcohol smell, extremely runny texture, and tingling were not for me. Caudalie's little yellow tube was genuinely refreshing by comparison.

The Caudalie formula has no noticeable scent, does not sting, and leaves my skin with a very pretty glow reminiscent of the Josie Maran finish I am still chasing. It is not as runny as the La Roche-Posay fluid, although it is still runnier than I ideally want a sunscreen to be. Apparently I would like SPF50 protection in the texture of a dependable moisturizer, which should not be an outrageous request.

It applies easily, feels comfortable, and gives me the sort of healthy finish that makes sunscreen feel like part of my skincare rather than protective paperwork. Most importantly, my skin seems to appreciate the formula.

### What the Internet Thinks

Caudalie's European site currently shows a 4.6 out of 5 rating from more than 370 reviews, with reviewers frequently praising its light feel, invisible finish, and wear under makeup. The brand does award loyalty points for submitting a review, which is worth keeping in mind.

The less enthusiastic reviews mention pilling, shine, or a smell despite the current formula being fragrance-free. Reddit is similarly divided: one [recent review](https://www.reddit.com/r/SkincareAddiction/comments/1ulk01m/review_my_take_on_these_two_sunscreens/) found it lightweight and non-irritating but too oily for combination skin. This may be one of those sunscreens where my preferred glow is someone else's grease.

### Verdict

This is one of the better European sunscreens I tried this summer. I would buy it again while I can easily get it from a French pharmacy, particularly for travel or everyday wear when I want a comfortable, glowy finish. The texture is not perfect, but it is close enough—and no burning is an exceptionally persuasive feature after my last experiment.

It is best suited to normal or dry skin and anyone who likes a luminous finish without fragrance or a white cast. Very oily skin may find it too shiny, and anyone who insists on a mineral formula should look at the U.S. Caudalie alternative below.

### Alternatives and How They Differ

**[Caudalie Daily Mineral Sunscreen Broad Spectrum SPF 50 Lotion](https://us.caudalie.com/p/445A/445a-daily-mineral-sunscreen-broad-spectrum-spf-50-lotion.html)** is the U.S. counterpart, but not the same formula—and I have only tried the European one. It costs $39 for 50 ml and uses 21.1% non-nano zinc oxide, plus niacinamide and Viniferine to address the appearance of dark spots. It is fragrance-free, glowy, and pregnancy-safe according to Caudalie, but the brand acknowledges that a mineral white cast can be more noticeable on deeper skin tones. This is the formula I want to try next.

**[Clinique UV Solutions Brightening + Tinted Sunscreen SPF 50/PA+++](https://www.ulta.com/p/uv-solutions-brightening-tinted-sunscreen-broad-spectrum-spf-50pa-pimprod2058929)** costs $44 for 1.35 oz. It is another U.S.-available mineral SPF50 with niacinamide, dark-spot claims, and a glowy finish. Unlike either Caudalie formula, it is tinted and comes in four shades, so choosing the right match matters.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is the European Caudalie SPF50+ a mineral sunscreen?

No. The current formula uses four organic or chemical UV filters and contains no zinc oxide or titanium dioxide.

#### Can You Buy This Formula in the United States?

Not through Caudalie's current U.S. storefront. U.S. shoppers are offered the separate Daily Mineral Sunscreen SPF 50, while the European Lightweight Cream may occasionally be available through importers.

#### Is It Pregnancy Safe?

Yes, according to Caudalie. The brand's [U.K. sun-care collection](https://uk.caudalie.com/c/all-products/needs/sun-protection.html), which includes this exact Lightweight Cream, says the range is suitable for pregnant women. Your clinician should still review your complete routine if you have individual concerns.
