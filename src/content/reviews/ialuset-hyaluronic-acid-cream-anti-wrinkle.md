### What Is Ialuset Hyaluronic Acid Cream?

[Ialuset Hyaluronic Acid Cream](https://frenchpharmacy.com/products/hyaluronic-acid-cream-anti-wrinkle) is one of those French-pharmacy products that migrated from the medicine cabinet to the skincare shelf. The 100-gram tube contains 0.2% sodium hyaluronate, plus glycerin and a cream base. In France, however, it is a medical device for wound healing—not an anti-wrinkle cosmetic. The [current patient leaflet](https://www.ibsafrance.fr/dam/jcr:ac44a49b-6bb4-4613-803d-bb61cdddd151/Notice%20ialuset.2025-01-28-10-12-32.pdf) says not to use it cosmetically, so applying it as face cream is off-label.

Hyaluronic acid is a humectant: it binds water and helps skin feel hydrated, soft, and temporarily plumper. That can make dehydration lines less obvious, but I would not put it in the same long-term anti-aging category as sunscreen or a retinoid. A [review of topical hyaluronic acid](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10078143/) supports its hydration benefits, while its role in maintaining a moist environment explains the wound-care origin.

### My Thoughts on Ialuset Hyaluronic Acid Cream

I am a big fan of hyaluronic acid and what hydration can do for the way skin ages. I had heard about Ialuset as a cult French-pharmacy wound cream and beauty secret. Naturally, I had to try it.

For me, the trick is giving it water to hold. I mist my face, apply a thin layer, mist again if I am feeling especially parched, then follow with an emollient moisturizer, facial oil, or my [Egyptian Magic slugging layer](https://theskinroutine.com/blog/egyptian-magic-all-purpose-skin-cream/). Mist, Ialuset, mist, seal—it is like giving my skin a very large drink of water and then refusing to let it escape.

Used this way, it leaves my skin feeling plush by morning. Used on dry skin without anything richer on top, it can do the opposite and leave me feeling tight. Damp skin and a sealing layer are absolutely what make Ialuset work for me.

### How I Use Ialuset

I use a small amount on clean, damp skin, then seal it with emollient or occlusive ingredients. To buffer tretinoin, I apply a thin layer and let my skin dry completely before the prescription goes on. The [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/acne/skin-care/moisturizer) notes that moisturizer can help skin tolerate retinoids, but I avoid trapping tretinoin under a heavy slugging layer because extra occlusion may mean extra irritation.

Its wound-healing purpose is a useful bonus, but I would follow the leaflet rather than improvising on a deep, infected, or slow-healing wound. I also would not touch the tube opening directly to broken skin.

### What the Internet Thinks about Ialuset

The U.S. retailer shows hundreds of reviews, overwhelmingly at five stars. Fans describe bouncier, smoother skin and a cream more substantial than a watery HA serum. Others find it heavy, experience pilling, or say too much hyaluronic acid makes their skin feel drier—another reason the layers around it matter.

### Would I Recommend Ialuset Hyaluronic Acid Cream?

Yes, with a large asterisk. I love it as a nighttime humectant, especially under Egyptian Magic, but this is an off-label cosmetic use of a wound-care product. Dry, dehydrated skin will appreciate it most; acne-prone or highly reactive skin may prefer something formulated as facial skincare.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can I Use Ialuset with Tretinoin?

I use a thin layer as a moisturizer buffer, then wait until my skin is fully dry before applying tretinoin. Start cautiously, follow your prescriber's directions, and do not assume that adding a heavy occlusive on top is automatically better.

#### Is Ialuset Pregnancy Safe?

The current Ialuset leaflet tells pregnant or breastfeeding users to ask a doctor or pharmacist before use. Hyaluronic acid itself is not the concern, but because this exact medical-device formula does not provide an unconditional pregnancy-use recommendation—and facial use is off-label—I have marked it as not verified for pregnancy on this site.

#### Can I Use Ialuset as a Regular Moisturizer?

I do, but it behaves more like a concentrated hydration layer than a complete moisturizer for me. Apply a small amount to damp skin and follow with an emollient cream, oil, or occlusive if your skin still feels tight.
