### What Is LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask?

[LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask](https://us.laneige.com/products/lip-sleeping-mask) hardly needs an introduction. It is the viral K-beauty lip mask that became a cult classic and somehow made sleeping in lip balm feel like a proper beauty ritual. The original Berry version costs $24 for a very generous 20 g jar.

The formula is designed to soften, smooth, and protect dry lips overnight. It combines glycerin with coconut oil, shea butter, and murumuru seed butter, then wraps everything in a glossy layer of emollients, polymers, and waxes. In practical terms, it is an occlusive product: it creates a film that helps slow moisture loss while you sleep. LANEIGE reports 12-hour hydration after one use in a 33-person instrumental study.

It does not contain mineral oil, petrolatum, or lanolin. It does contain petroleum-derived synthetic ingredients such as hydrogenated polyisobutene, polybutene, and microcrystalline wax. That distinction matters if you have an ingredient preference, but it does not make properly refined cosmetic petrolatum unsafe.

### My Thoughts on LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask

This product really is that girl. It has lived beside my bed for years, and I have no idea how many empties I have finished. I apply it before bed and wake up with lips that feel hydrated, soft, and plump. Because I am not eating, drinking, or talking in my sleep, the glossy layer has a much better chance of staying where I put it than any lip product I apply during the day.

Berry is the obvious classic, but Mint Choco was my ultimate favorite and is much harder to find these days. Gummy Bear was not for me—it smelled like grape cough syrup. Scent is very personal, and this line offers enough flavors to turn choosing one into a minor personality test.

I have never broken out around my mouth from it, despite the rich texture. The tub also lasts an almost comical amount of time. Mine lasted around two years with nightly use, which makes the price much easier to justify. However, the open-jar symbol indicates a 12-month period after opening. The often-quoted three-year shelf life applies to an unopened product, not three years after you first dip into it, so I would follow the date and symbol printed on your own packaging.

### What the Internet Thinks about LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask

Sephora has collected more than 22,000 reviews, with shoppers most often praising the scent, softness, and texture. The internet also agrees on one thing: a 20 g jar lasts forever. In [Reddit discussions](https://www.reddit.com/r/Sephora/comments/1b5uxqi/is_the_laniege_sleep_mask_and_lip_gloss_actually/), people report finishing a jar anywhere from about ten months to two years, which helps explain why many consider $24 reasonable.

The disagreement is about performance. Fans wake up with softer lips and say the mask outlasts basic balms. Critics feel it sits on top without actually helping, find the fragrance too strong, or say their lips feel drier later. Flavor opinions are equally chaotic: one recent [collection ranking](https://www.reddit.com/r/Sephora/comments/1qa3qwo/my_entire_laneige_collection_ranked/) called Berry nostalgic and bubble-gum-like while describing Gummy Bear as grape medicine—so at least I am not alone.

### Would I Recommend LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask?

Absolutely. I will keep buying it because it is comfortable, effective for me, and one jar lasts long enough to make a prestige lip product feel surprisingly sensible. I would start with Berry if you want the classic experience.

I would skip it if you are fragrance-sensitive, dislike jars, or already know that rich, film-forming lip products make your lips feel worse. In that case, a plain unscented ointment may be the more dependable choice.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can You Use It During the Day?

Yes. LANEIGE recommends a thin layer during the day or a generous layer before bed. I prefer it overnight because it stays put longer.

#### How Long Does a Jar Last?

Mine lasted about two years, while online reports vary from under a year to around two. For product safety, follow the 12M period-after-opening symbol on your jar rather than treating personal empty timelines as shelf-life advice.

#### Is LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask Pregnancy-Safe?

Yes, based on the current Berry ingredient list, which does not contain retinoids or another obvious pregnancy-restricted active. LANEIGE does not specifically certify its products for pregnancy or breastfeeding and advises checking with your physician, particularly if you have individual sensitivities or concerns.
