### What Is Supergoop! Bright-Eyed 100% Mineral Eye Cream SPF 40?

[Supergoop! Bright-Eyed 100% Mineral Eye Cream SPF 40](https://supergoop.com/products/bright-eyed-100-mineral-eye-cream-spf-40) is a daytime eye cream, mineral sunscreen, and subtle color corrector in one 0.5-ounce bottle. It costs $40 and uses 14.4% zinc oxide for broad-spectrum SPF 40 protection. Supergoop! also includes caffeine, probiotics, pomegranate, and green tea extract, with claims around brightening and visibly reducing puffiness.

The formula has a lightly illuminating pink tint intended to counteract tired-looking undereyes and soften the usual white cast of mineral SPF. It is made for the thin skin around the eyes, where many regular sunscreens migrate, sting, or simply feel unpleasant enough that people avoid the area altogether.

### My Thoughts on Supergoop! Bright-Eyed 100% Mineral Eye Cream SPF 40

I have used this every day for years. I genuinely do not know how many empties I have finished, which is either an excellent endorsement or evidence that I should improve my household inventory system. It is the best eye cream with SPF I have ever used.

The usual complaint about sunscreen around the eyes is burning. Chemical sunscreens are often the culprit for sensitive eyes, and the skin here is so delicate that it is not surprising many people struggle to protect it properly. Mineral SPF tends to be better tolerated, but then you may have to negotiate with a white cast. Bright-Eyed avoids both problems for me: it does not sting, and its tint makes my undereye area look more awake rather than chalky.

I also appreciate that this is not sunscreen pretending to be an eye cream. The texture feels comfortable, the caffeine is a sensible addition for morning puffiness, and the slight pearly tint gives immediate brightness without requiring concealer.

Shade flexibility is my only real hesitation when recommending it universally. I have light-medium olive skin and the tint works beautifully for me, but one pink shade cannot realistically be perfect for everyone. I would swatch it first if you are extremely fair or have a rich deep skin tone.

### What the Internet Thinks about Supergoop! Bright-Eyed 100% Mineral Eye Cream SPF 40

The broader response is positive, though not as devoted as mine. Supergoop!’s site shows roughly 4.3 out of 5 stars from more than 870 reviews, while [Ulta](https://www.ulta.com/p/bright-eyed-100-mineral-eye-cream-spf-40-pimprod2036474) lists about 4.1 stars from more than 700 reviews.

The complaints are consistent too: some reviewers find the tint too pink, the finish streaky, or the mineral texture pasty—especially over dry skin or under concealer. In a [Reddit discussion about eye-area sunscreen](https://www.reddit.com/r/SkincareAddiction/comments/1f9bchi/sun_care_mission_impossible_affordable_sunscreen/), experiences ranged from reliable non-stinging protection to poor spreadability and irritation. Mineral does not automatically mean universally gentle, unfortunately; skin refuses to respect neat categories.

### Would I recommend Supergoop! Bright-Eyed 100% Mineral Eye Cream SPF 40?

I will keep buying this. It protects an area that is easy to neglect, never burns my eyes, brightens without looking like makeup on my skin, and replaces a separate daytime eye cream. After years of daily use and an uncountable number of empties, this is not a tentative recommendation. It is a permanent resident.

I would especially recommend it to someone with sensitive eyes who finds regular facial sunscreen uncomfortable around them. Just keep the single tint in mind and patch test if your eye area is particularly reactive.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Does Supergoop! Bright-Eyed sting the eyes?

It has never stung mine, which is one of the main reasons I remain loyal to it. Individual reactions vary, and the label still says to keep the product out of the eyes.

#### Can it replace concealer?

It brightens and lightly color-corrects, but it is not full coverage. On me, it is enough for an everyday, no-makeup look.

#### Can I wear it under makeup?

Yes. Let it set before applying concealer, and moisturize first if your undereye area is dry to reduce streaking or pilling.
