### What Is Egyptian Magic All-Purpose Skin Cream?

[Egyptian Magic All-Purpose Skin Cream](https://www.egyptianmagic.com/product) is a waterless balm made from five ingredients: olive oil, beeswax, honey, royal jelly, and propolis. The four-ounce jar is $39. According to the [brand's history](https://www.egyptianmagic.com/#about), its founder learned the basis of the recipe after meeting a stranger in a Chicago diner in 1986, then began hand-pouring jars in 1991. The packaging has barely changed, and neither has its mystique.

It helps to understand the three jobs a moisturizer can perform. Humectants attract and retain water—think glycerin and hyaluronic acid. Emollients, including squalane, oils, and ceramides, soften and smooth. Occlusives, such as petrolatum and waxes, form a surface barrier that reduces water loss. Many moisturizers combine all three. Here, olive oil is primarily the emollient while beeswax provides much of the occlusive seal.

Slugging simply means applying an occlusive as the final nighttime step. The ritual long predates TikTok, with roots in Black households, Korean skincare, and other communities of color. [Dictionary.com](https://www.dictionary.com/culture/fashion/slugging) traces the term on Reddit to at least 2014. Social interest took off in late 2020 and peaked through 2021–2022; [Vaseline conversation](https://www.personalcareinsights.com/news/tiktok-slugging-trend-vaseline-drives-protecting-and-healing-skincare-markets.html) in January 2022 was reportedly triple its February 2021 level. A [2022 dermatology study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35961481/) analyzed 49 leading English-language TikToks that had collectively passed 26.5 million views. Only 37% adequately discussed benefits and risks.

### My Thoughts on Egyptian Magic All-Purpose Skin Cream

When slugging was everywhere about five years ago, I knew I wanted it in my routine. Keeping moisture in is one of the most reliable ways to support how skin looks as it ages. I was not, however, about to slather petrolatum all over my face, so I went looking for a more natural alternative. Enter Egyptian Magic.

It is not as aggressively occlusive as Vaseline, but that is not necessarily a loss. A heavy seal over retinoids or exfoliating acids can intensify irritation. I save slugging for right before bed and let some time pass after my actives and moisturizer rather than immediately trapping everything underneath.

Right before Egyptian Magic, I apply [Ialuset Hyaluronic Acid Cream Anti Wrinkle](https://frenchpharmacy.com/collections/ialuset/products/hyaluronic-acid-cream-anti-wrinkle). Egyptian Magic contains no water, so I want a humectant-rich layer underneath for it to seal in. Then I warm a small amount between my fingers and press it over my face.

I go to bed looking greasy, but I never wake up dry—even in winter with the heat pumping. By morning it does not feel as though the routine has migrated into my hair and pillowcase. That medium-weight seal is exactly why it works for me.

### What the Internet Thinks about Egyptian Magic

The official site reports 4.6 stars across more than 20,000 verified Amazon reviews. Fans use it for dry patches, lips, cuticles, makeup removal, and full-face moisture, noting that the jar lasts forever. [Reddit is more divided](https://www.reddit.com/r/Skincare_Addiction/comments/15p30qh): some call it a dry-skin and makeup-kit staple; others report breakouts, find it too greasy, or question the price of such a short ingredient list.

Propolis can also cause allergic contact dermatitis. A bee-sting allergy does not automatically prove a topical propolis allergy, but anyone with known propolis, honey, or beeswax sensitivity should skip it. Patch testing is sensible for reactive skin.

### Would I Recommend Egyptian Magic All-Purpose Skin Cream?

Yes. I keep repurchasing it because it gives dry skin a comfortable overnight seal without petrolatum's full force. It is best for normal-to-dry skin and dedicated sluggers who appreciate a balm. Acne-prone skin may prefer spot-slugging first, and anyone using a retinoid should proceed with caution, but in my experience, letting some time pass between actives and slugging hasn't caused any ill effects.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Should Egyptian Magic Go Over Retinol?

I do not apply it immediately over retinoids or strong acids. Occlusion may increase irritation, so separate the steps, use it on recovery nights, or ask your dermatologist how to combine them.

#### Is Egyptian Magic Pregnancy Safe?

Under this site's ingredient policy, the current five-ingredient formula is pregnancy-safe. Check your jar for formula changes and ask your clinician about individual allergies or concerns.

#### Can Acne-Prone Skin Use Egyptian Magic?

Possibly, but a full-face layer may feel too heavy or trigger breakouts for some people. Patch test and begin on dry areas rather than slugging everywhere.
