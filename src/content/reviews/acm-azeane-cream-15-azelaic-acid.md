### What Is ACM Azéane Cream 15% Azelaic Acid?

[ACM Azéane Cream 15% Azelaic Acid](https://labo-acm.com/en/products/azeane-cream-azelaic-acid-15) is a French-pharmacy treatment for blemishes, redness, dark spots, texture, and dullness. The 30 ml tube costs €22.20 in France. US readers can [import it through Care to Beauty](https://www.caretobeauty.com/us/acm-laboratoire-azeane-cream-15-azelaic-acid-30ml/) for about $34 before shipping.

The fragrance-free cream combines 15% azelaic acid with niacinamide, glycerin, and xylitol. ACM says to use it morning and/or evening before moisturizer and sunscreen. Its satisfaction studies reported improvements in spots, texture, redness, and radiance, although they included only 33 and 21 volunteers. Useful context before we turn percentages into gospel.

### My Thoughts on ACM Azéane Cream 15% Azelaic Acid

This is my first experience with azelaic acid. I bought it because I was in France, kept seeing it recommended as a pharmacy find, and it was inexpensive enough to inspire a very scientific “why not?”

Initially, I did not think I was the target user. I have little redness and am no longer especially acne-prone. Then I learned azelaic acid also targets dark spots, dullness, and texture—three things I noticed postpartum.

Azelaic acid is difficult to formulate elegantly. Many creams and suspensions feel gritty, sandy, or inclined to roll off your face in tiny eraser shavings. Azéane feels like an actual nice cream: smooth, comfortable, and easy to layer. It does not sting or burn on me, even after vitamin C, although that tolerance is personal rather than a challenge for everyone else to copy.

Unfortunately, I made it almost impossible to determine exactly what it is doing. Around the same time, I restarted tretinoin and vitamin C. Once the postpartum glow faded, I contemplated the months of skincare I had skipped during pregnancy and breastfeeding and naturally threw the kitchen sink at my face.

My skin tone looks brighter and more even, the texture has smoothed out, and my forehead lines seem less etched. I cannot assign those changes to Azéane alone, but it fits without irritation, pilling, or drama. The active that layers easily is the active I will consistently use.

I wish I had discovered azelaic acid before pregnancy. ACM says this cream can be used while pregnant or breastfeeding, and [ACOG includes azelaic acid](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/skin-conditions-during-pregnancy) among topical ingredients that can be used during pregnancy. Personal medical questions still belong with your healthcare provider.

### What the Internet Thinks about ACM Azéane Cream 15% Azelaic Acid

ACM’s website currently shows 4.61 out of 5 from 157 reviews. The broader discussion sounds very similar to my experience: users frequently praise the smooth cream texture, matte finish, lack of pilling, and gradual improvements in tone, post-acne marks, and texture.

In a detailed [EuroSkincare reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/EuroSkincare/comments/1hsqxma/did_anyone_try_acm_azeane_15_azelaic_acid_cream/), some called it their favorite formula and reported no stinging. Others found it heavy, experienced itching, or suspected the niacinamide contributed to breakouts. Fifteen percent is still meaningful; pleasant texture does not make irritation impossible.

### Would I Recommend ACM Azéane Cream 15% Azelaic Acid?

Yes. It is a morning staple because it gives me higher-strength azelaic acid without the gritty texture I expected. I would recommend it for dark spots, dullness, texture, redness, or blemishes—especially if elegant layering matters.

Introduce it gradually on dry skin if you are sensitive, and do not add it alongside several other actives if you want any hope of knowing what works. I offer this advice from the wreckage of my own experiment design.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can You Use Azéane During Pregnancy or Breastfeeding?

ACM explicitly says yes, and ACOG lists azelaic acid as an ingredient that can be used during pregnancy. Confirm your complete routine with your own healthcare provider.

#### Can You Use Azéane with Vitamin C?

I use them together comfortably in the morning. Introduce one product at a time and separate them if the combination causes irritation.

#### Does Azéane Replace Moisturizer?

ACM positions it before moisturizer. Whether you need another layer depends on your skin and the season.
