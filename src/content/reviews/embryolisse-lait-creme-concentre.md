### What Is Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré?

[Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré](https://us.embryolisse.com/products/lait-creme-concentre-daily-face-and-body-cream) is a $32 French pharmacy cream. Embryolisse calls it a six-in-one product: moisturizer, makeup base, cleansing milk, moisturizing mask, after-shave care, and after-sun care. The 2.54-ounce tube is marketed for all skin types and as non-comedogenic.

The formula pairs mineral oil with shea butter, beeswax, aloe vera, and soy proteins to soften skin and reduce moisture loss. It also contains fragrance, described by the brand as sweet and floral. I initially hesitated because Yuka gave my original cream 47/100 while the Sensitive version scored 93/100. Fragrance is part of that story, but the current [Lait-Crème Sensitive](https://us.embryolisse.com/products/lait-creme-sensitive-fragrance-free) has a substantially different formula. Yuka's cosmetic scores do not account for ingredient concentration or efficacy, so I do not treat one number as the final word.

### My Thoughts on Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré

Luckily, I am not particularly sensitive to fragrance, and the scent is mild enough that it has never bothered me. I still would have bought the Sensitive version if I could have found it. For such a cult favorite, either Embryolisse moisturizer was surprisingly difficult to track down in the French pharmacies I visited in Dijon and Colmar.

I expected the moisturizer and soothing claims; after-shave and after-sun care feel like fairly obvious variations on the same job. Cleansing milk and makeup base were more intriguing. I knew this was a staple in many makeup artists' kits, but I did not think I would personally use it that way—until I did.

In winter my skin can get a bit dry, and when a lot of time has passed between my morning skincare and makeup the skin under my eyes gets a little dry, and concealer settles into every fine line it can find. A tiny amount of Lait-Crème Concentré adds just enough moisture for a smoother base. Let it settle for a few minutes before applying makeup.

I also use it as a normal morning or evening moisturizer when my skin needs a little extra oomph. The texture feels comforting without requiring a complicated rescue routine. Using it as cleansing milk still feels like washing a perfectly good moisturizer down the drain, but in a pinch—or while traveling with limited space—I understand the appeal.

### What the Internet Thinks about Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré

The feedback is skin-dependent. At the time of writing, [Ulta shoppers](https://www.ulta.com/p/lait-creme-concentre-multi-function-moisturizer-pimprod2053340) rate it around 4.3 stars, although the review pool is small. Fans praise the soft finish and how makeup sits over it.

In [Reddit discussions](https://www.reddit.com/r/EuroSkincare/comments/1irsjr8/thoughts_on_embryolisse_laitcr%C3%A9m%C3%A9_concentr%C3%A9/), people repeatedly mention winter hydration, makeup prep, and the fact that the fragrance fades. The less enthusiastic reviews say it caused breakouts, was not rich enough for serious barrier repair, or did not cooperate with their foundation. That sounds right for a fragranced, occlusive-leaning cream: lovely for many people, but not automatically universal because makeup artists love it.

### Would I Recommend Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré?

Yes, especially for normal-to-dry skin, seasonal dehydration, or makeup that needs a little more cushion underneath. It won me over as a practical multitasker rather than a miracle in a tube. I would choose the Sensitive version if you avoid fragrance or have reactive skin, and I would patch test either formula if you are acne-prone.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré a Moisturizer or a Primer?

It can be both. Use a small amount as moisturizer, or smooth a thin layer over dry areas and wait two to three minutes before makeup.

#### Can It Really Remove Makeup?

Embryolisse says it can be used generously as a cleansing milk. I would reserve that trick for light makeup or travel and follow with a proper cleanse when needed.

#### Is Embryolisse Lait-Crème Concentré Pregnancy Safe?

Under this site's ingredient policy, yes: the current published formula does not list retinoids, hydroquinone, or the exfoliating acids we flag during pregnancy. Formulas can change, so check your tube and ask your clinician if you have individual concerns.
